# Positioning
Checked: 2026-11-06

## Buyer job

On Diwali night, burst Indian crackers with the family, with real-sounding bangs, without smoke, at a volume that suits whoever is home (a baby, a dog, a grandparent, neighbours).

## Incumbent

Real crackers. Online, the most complete browser alternative is Virtual Diwali (virtualdiwali.com).

## Scenario

One family, one phone, Diwali night, wanting to burst crackers without smoke, with someone at home who'd rather it wasn't too loud.

## Our price

₹0. No ads, no login, no in-app purchase. MIT licensed. Hosting is one Cloudflare Worker.

## Three ways we are better

1. **Real recorded bangs.** Every bomb, bijli and ladi pop plays a CC0 or public-domain field recording, pitched and panned per blast. Faris Farooq Rah's simulator: "There are no recordings." Source: farisfarooq.com page above.
2. **A volume slider, not just a mute button.** 0 to 100, remembered on the device. Virtual Diwali: "mute it from the top corner". OXAAM: "use the sound button to switch it off". Sources: their pages above.
3. **Fire at will, and read the code.** All 12 crackers from the first tap, no boxes to find, no rounds, no paid greeting, MIT source. Virtual Diwali has 16 hidden boxes, OXAAM rounds of 20, ShareMyInvite "One payment". None of the four lists source code.

## Where we are worse

- Virtual Diwali is a full 3D world with diyas and a shared lantern sky. We are one street.
- Faris Farooq Rah lets you write a wish with the phuljhadi and share it as a link. We have no sharing.
- ShareMyInvite makes a personalised greeting. We don't make greetings.
- OXAAM has a score. We have no game.

## Claims we will not make

- "Most realistic" or "best" anything.
- Any pollution figure we "saved". We can't measure what someone didn't burst.
- Anything about other sites' sound sources beyond what their pages say.
- That virtual crackers replace the festival, or that bursting real crackers is wrong.
- User counts or testimonials we don't have.
